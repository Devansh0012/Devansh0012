### About

I spend most of my time learning about systems and figuring out why something that *should* work… doesn’t.

Currently building at **Portkey AI**.  
Mostly working around APIs, infra, and the fun parts of networking that only show up in production.

I enjoy:
- tracing bugs across layers until they stop hiding  
- making systems a little more predictable than they were yesterday  
- removing unnecessary complexity (and occasionally adding the right kind)

### Selected work

- **Eddie** *(private)*: a local voice assistant for macOS, inspired by JARVIS/FRIDAY. A Node.js agent with a native Swift layer for speech and audio, interruptible by voice, and integrated with Calendar, Reminders, Contacts and confirmed iMessage/WhatsApp sends. It answers in English and Hinglish, and reaches the iPhone through Siri Shortcuts over Tailscale.
- **[Braventex](https://github.com/Devansh0012/social-app)**: a student-only community and collaboration platform with communities, a feed, study rooms and realtime notifications. Built as a modular monolith on Fastify + Mercurius (GraphQL), Prisma/PostgreSQL and WebSockets, with a Next.js 15 frontend.
- **[violence-detection](https://github.com/Devansh0012/violence-detection)**: real-time violence detection on RTSP/webcam streams. A PyTorch MobileNetV2 classifier with Faster R-CNN and MediaPipe for people and pose, served over FastAPI WebSockets, with detections stored in MongoDB and clips uploaded to S3.
- **[devansh-portfolio](https://github.com/Devansh0012/devansh-portfolio)**: [devanshdubey.com](https://www.devanshdubey.com/), built on Next.js with an MDX blog, RSS and email subscriptions, and interactive demos running C++ kernels compiled to WebAssembly.
